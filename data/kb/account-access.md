# Account Access and Login Problems

Password reset: use the "Forgot password" link on the login page. The reset email arrives within 5 minutes and the link expires after 60 minutes.

Account lockout: after 5 failed login attempts the account is locked for 30 minutes. Agents can unlock an account immediately after verifying the customer's email address.

Two-factor authentication problems: agents can disable 2FA only after identity verification. Never share or reset passwords directly in a reply.
