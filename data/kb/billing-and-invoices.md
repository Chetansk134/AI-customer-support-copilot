# Billing and Invoices

Invoices are generated on the due date each month and emailed to the account owner. A PDF copy of any past invoice can be downloaded from Billing > Invoice History, or the agent can resend it by email.

Failed payments are retried automatically after 3 days and again after 7 days. After the second failed retry the invoice is marked overdue and the account becomes past due.

Past-due accounts keep read-only access for 14 days. After that the account is suspended until the overdue balance is paid. Paying the balance restores access within 15 minutes.
