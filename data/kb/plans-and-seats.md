# Plans and Seats

Plans: Starter ($9/month, 1 seat), Pro ($49/month, up to 10 seats), Enterprise (custom pricing, unlimited seats).

Adding seats: on Pro, each extra seat beyond 10 requires moving to Enterprise. Seat changes on Enterprise are billed pro rata for the remainder of the billing period and appear on the next invoice.

Upgrades take effect immediately. Downgrades take effect at the end of the current billing period.
