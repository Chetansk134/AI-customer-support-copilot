# Refund Policy

Customers can request a refund within 30 days of a charge. Refunds go back to the original payment method and take 5-7 business days to appear.

Duplicate charges: if a customer was charged twice for the same billing period, the duplicate charge is refunded in full, no questions asked. Agents should confirm both charges in the billing system first.

Refunds on annual plans are prorated after the first 30 days. Enterprise contracts follow the terms in the signed agreement; escalate these to the account manager.
